Hello everyone, I am [**Harsh Trivedi**](https://www.facebook.com/harsh98trivedi){:target="_blank"}, a technology enthusiast, developer, designer, and a **{{ 'now' | date: "%Y" | minus: 1999 }}**-year-old guy who loves exploring almost every aspect of *Technology*. I was born and brought up in a lovely city, **Kanpur** *(formerly Cawnpore)*, located in the central part of **Uttar Pradesh, India**. I love Graphic Designing, Web Development, Video Editing, Travelling, exploring new technologies, learning new things, and meeting people with similar interests and mindsets.

*Computer* has been my favourite subject since childhood. It has completely changed the way I think, approach problems, and understand the world around me. The best part about working with computers is the learning experience. Whether you are designing something, editing a video, developing a website, or programming an application, there is always something new to learn. When you work towards a goal with curiosity and dedication, you gradually understand how things work and how you can experiment with them or tweak them in your own way.

I have always enjoyed experimenting with technology, exploring software, and finding ways to make things work better. I love spending time with design tools, browsing open-source repositories, tinkering with the systems I own, and watching technical content from some of my favourite creators. Building something from scratch, solving a complicated technical problem, or optimising an existing web application gives me a different kind of satisfaction.

I started this [**Jekyll**](https://jekyllrb.com){:target="_blank"} based blog to share my thoughts, experiences, knowledge, experiments, and pretty much anything interesting that I come across. You will find posts about technology, programming, web development, graphic designing, software, open-source projects, troubleshooting, and other things that I enjoy exploring. I will also share some of the applications, websites, browser extensions, and tools that I have developed or use in my everyday workflow.

### A little about my professional journey

My professional journey started with a strong interest in computers and web technologies, which gradually turned into a career in software development and technical consulting. Over time, I have worked across different areas of the technology industry, including web development, enterprise technical support, software troubleshooting, quality assurance, and cloud infrastructure.

I have worked with technologies and platforms such as JavaScript, React, Svelte, Next.js, HTML, CSS, Python, PHP, WordPress, SQL, MySQL, REST APIs, and various cloud and CDN technologies.

I have also worked with international clients and enterprise organisations, helping them troubleshoot complex technical issues, understand application behaviour, resolve infrastructure problems, and improve their overall experience with software products. My experience in technical support has taught me to look beyond the visible problem, investigate the underlying cause, read documentation, experiment, and find practical solutions.

I particularly enjoy frontend development because it brings together two things I love: programming and creativity. Designing an interface and then bringing it to life with code is something I genuinely enjoy.

### Design, development, and everything in between

Apart from my professional work, I have been freelancing as a Web Developer, Graphic Designer, and Content Creator since 2015. During this time, I have designed and developed more than 20 websites and created visual assets for different clients.

I enjoy working with tools like Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Figma. Whether it is designing a logo, creating a social media graphic, developing a website layout, or editing a video, I like experimenting with different creative approaches.

I also enjoy working on my own digital projects. Some of my work includes WordPress plugins, Chrome extensions, web applications, developer utilities, and small experimental projects.

A few of my projects include:

- [**Romeo Redirect Manager**](https://wordpress.org/plugins/romeo-redirect-manager/): A WordPress plugin for managing URL redirects with features like bulk management and slug conflict detection.
- [**Juliet Just Masks**](https://wordpress.org/plugins/juliet-just-masks/): A WordPress plugin for URL masking and reverse proxy routing.
- [**EchoFolio**](https://github.com/harsh98trivedi/EchoFolio): A portfolio theme for developers and designers.
- [**MultiFormat Data Converter**](https://github.com/harsh98trivedi/MultiFormat-Data-Converter): A privacy-focused data conversion tool built with Svelte.
- [**One-Click HAR Exporter**](https://github.com/harsh98trivedi/One-Click-HAR-Exporter): A browser utility that makes exporting HAR network logs easier.
- [**Smart Content Blocker**](https://github.com/harsh98trivedi/Smart-Content-Blocker): A Chrome extension that helps block inappropriate content and social media distractions.
- [**6174 Constant**](https://github.com/harsh98trivedi/6174-Constant): An interactive experiment exploring the famous Kaprekar constant.

I have also published several browser extensions on the Chrome Web Store, including YouTube Quick Controls, Amazon Search History Cleaner, and YouTube Studio One-Click Liker.

You can find more of my experiments and source code on [**GitHub**](https://github.com/harsh98trivedi){:target="_blank"}.

### Open source and WordPress

I have been contributing to the [**WordPress.org**](https://wordpress.org){:target="_blank"} ecosystem since August 2017. Over the years, I have explored WordPress plugin development, theme development, Gutenberg, Full Site Editing, WooCommerce customisation, REST API integrations, and other parts of the WordPress ecosystem.

I enjoy exploring how open-source software works internally, reading source code, finding bugs, and building tools that solve everyday problems. Developing and maintaining my own plugins has also helped me understand software architecture, compatibility, debugging, and the importance of writing maintainable code.

WordPress has been a big part of my development journey, and I continue to enjoy experimenting with it.

### Beyond the computer screen

Although technology takes up a major part of my life, I also enjoy travelling, photography, video editing, and exploring new places. I like meeting people, exchanging ideas, and learning from their experiences.

I have also volunteered at several technology events in different roles, including web development, graphic designing, photography, social media management, and event production. These experiences have allowed me to explore different creative responsibilities and work with people who share an interest in technology.

I believe there is always something new to learn, regardless of how much experience you have. That is probably one of the reasons I never get bored of computers and technology.
